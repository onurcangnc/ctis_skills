# Evidence Policy

## Authority order

Apply this order when signals conflict:

1. Executable source that expresses deliberate project structure
2. Signed assignment specification or grading rubric
3. Lab guide paired with an official solution
4. Lecture material or worked example
5. Student submission or personal note
6. Inference from formatting, naming, or isolated examples

Generated scaffolding, build products, IDE state, databases, dependencies, and repository metadata are not style evidence.

## Confidence

- **High:** repeated in independent authoritative artifacts or confirmed by executable structure and specification.
- **Medium:** supported by more than one relevant artifact or one detailed authoritative artifact.
- **Limited:** supported by an isolated, visual-only, student-authored, ambiguous, or incomplete artifact.

Keep limited signals conservative. Use normal technical best practice for missing details and label it as general practice rather than corpus-derived behavior.

## Privacy and inference

Preserve course code and technical provenance only. Do not expose or reconstruct names, authorship metadata, biographies, emotional states, motives, diagnoses, or private thoughts. Communication tone may be described only as an observable property and must be tied to a concrete output behavior.
